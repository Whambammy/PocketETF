# PocketETF — The 1-Click Social Index Protocol on Solana

> **Wall Street indices, sized for your pocket and native to your social timeline.**

---

## 📌 Short Pitch
**PocketETF** turns any investment thesis into a tradeable, fractional equity index fund discovered and purchased directly inside social feeds in under 400ms. Powered by Solana Actions, Blinks, Pyth oracles, and Jupiter, PocketETF bridges social discovery and instant on-chain execution.

---

## 🚨 The Problem
Millions of retail investors discover stock ideas on X, Reddit, and Discord, but execution is broken:
* **Context Switching:** Acting on an analyst's thesis requires leaving feeds, opening a brokerage, and searching tickers.
* **Manual Math & Drag:** Building a thematic basket (e.g., 50% NVDA, 30% TSM, 20% AMD) requires calculating fractional shares and submitting multiple orders.
* **Fees & High Minimums:** Brokerages enforce deposit minimums, while traditional ETFs extract recurring expense ratios (0.20%–1.50%).
* **Siloed Equities & Gas Loss:** Tokenized stocks lack consumer indexing; multi-swaps risk 1232B MTU packet failure or burning gas on unfunded wallets.

---

## 💡 The Solution
PocketETF transforms thematic equity investing into a frictionless, 1-click social experience:
* **No-Code Studio:** Analysts assemble stock baskets in seconds, set weights, and generate a **Solana Blink URL**.
* **1-Click Social Discovery:** When users see a Blink on X, an interactive card renders holdings, weights, and Pyth confidence intervals. Clicking a preset ($5–$100) executes an atomic swap through Jupiter.
* **Direct Self-Custody:** Users receive underlying SPL stock tokens directly in their wallet with zero lockups, zero management fees, and Pyth NAV benchmarking.

---

## ⚡ How It Works

```
[Social Feed: X / Discord] 
       │ (1) Analyst shares Blink URL: pocketetf.app/etf/silicon-ai
       ▼
[Interactive Blink Card] 
       │ (2) User selects amount ($50) & clicks "Invest"
       ▼
[PocketETF Engine + Pyth & Jupiter] 
       │ (3) Checks USDC balance, computes Pyth NAV, routes swaps
       ▼
[Atomic Versioned Transaction (v0)] 
       │ (4) 1 biometric approval signs all swaps + ATA creations
       ▼
[User Wallet] ──── Settles all underlying SPL stocks in <400ms
```

1. **Curation:** Creators bundle 1:1 backed equities (Backpack/Sunrise) on Whirlpool, Meteora, and Raydium.
2. **Social Unfurl:** Solana Actions & Blinks convert the URL into an interactive card inside social timelines.
3. **Atomic Synthesis:** The backend validates on-chain USDC balances, computes Pyth NAV, and queries Jupiter v6 (`/swap-instructions`).
4. **Single-Tx Execution:** Swap instructions, compute budget, and idempotent ATAs compile into a `v0` transaction under 1232B.
5. **Instant Settlement:** The investor signs once; diversified equities settle in under 400ms.

## 🌟 Key Features
* **In-Feed Execution:** Invest directly on X without leaving the timeline via Solana Blinks.

* **Zero-Gas Guardrails:** Pre-flight balance checks block unfunded transactions, saving SOL.
* **Micro-Investing:** Put $5, $10, or $50 to work across multiple equities in 1 atomic v0 swap.
* **Zero Expense Ratios:** Free self-custody holding with no recurring management drag.
* **Permissionless Indexing:** Anyone can launch, share, and monetize custom indices.

## 🛠️ Why Solana
PocketETF is impossible on legacy finance or alternative blockchains:
* **Micro-Cent Fees:** Executing a multi-stock basket costs <**$0.002** on Solana (vs. $5–$25+ on EVM chains), making micro-investing mathematically viable.
* **Sub-Second Finality:** 400ms slots deliver the instant responsiveness expected in consumer social apps.
* **Blinks Distribution:** Solana Actions transform web links into interactive on-chain transaction interfaces across the web.
* **Address Lookup Tables (ALTs):** ALTs compress multi-hop Jupiter instructions to strictly respect Solana's 1232-byte MTU limit.

## 🏗️ Technical Architecture & Stack
* **Stack:** Next.js 14 App Router, TypeScript, Vanilla CSS, `@solana/wallet-adapter`.
* **Pyth Oracles:** Pyth Hermes v2 API, equity/crypto feed IDs, and real-time Basket NAV engine.
* **Routing:** Jupiter v6 (`/swap-instructions`) across Whirlpool, Meteora, and Raydium.
* **Solana Primitives:** Actions & Blinks Spec v2.1.3, Versioned Transactions (`v0`) with ALTs, idempotent ATAs, and dynamic priority fees.
